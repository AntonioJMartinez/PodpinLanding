# Outreach drafts — UNSENT

These are human-reviewed templates for relevant hosts, educators, editors, and independent reviewers. They are not an automation, mailing list, or permission to contact anyone. Do not send a draft until the recipient, public observation, product facts, and ask have been checked.

## Before personalizing

- Use a recipient who has recently published about podcasts, learning from audio, transcripts, notes, Apple apps, accessibility, or a closely related workflow.
- Record one public URL and one specific observation. Do not infer private circumstances, scrape personal details, or pretend to have listened to work you did not review.
- Explain why the topic is relevant to that recipient’s audience. A generic “great content” compliment is not personalization.
- Keep the ask small and optional. Offer a useful angle or neutral test brief, not a guaranteed story, link, review, or ranking.
- State any material relationship, access, loaner device, sponsorship, or affiliate arrangement before publication. Never pay for a positive review.
- Use the current App Store destination only: <https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760191862>.
- Include a simple opt-out. If there is no reply, do not keep following up. One follow-up is the maximum for these drafts.

## Send / do-not-send checklist

Send only when every applicable item is checked:

- [ ] The recipient’s current public work is relevant and the observation is linked.
- [ ] The draft uses only verified product facts and distinguishes on-device from optional cloud AI.
- [ ] The ask is optional, specific, and useful even if the recipient declines.
- [ ] Any access, compensation, affiliate, or relationship disclosure is ready.
- [ ] The recipient has an appropriate contact preference or has opted in where required.
- [ ] The message contains a clear opt-out and no pressure or artificial deadline.
- [ ] No review, rating, backlink, share, or coverage is promised or requested in exchange for a reward.
- [ ] A human owner has approved the exact recipient, subject, body, and send channel.

Do not send when the recipient has opted out, the public observation is stale or unverified, the message relies on an unsupported comparison, or the only proposed value is exposure for Podpin.
